Singapore Maritime Week

Singapore Maritime Week (SMW) is an annual flagship event organised by the Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore (MPA), SMW brings together global maritime leaders and industry captains to discuss key issues and developments in the sector. It is also a platform to demonstrate latest technologies and innovations, as well as to facilitate networking and collaboration among all stakeholders.

Introduction

Kiteboarding & Sailing gave us a visceral feel for the power of wind—and the inspiration to harness it

We show the world it's possible.

Since 2012, Shiprion has connected businesses and consumers across 180+ countries through air freight, sea freight, road haulage, and express courier services. We combine a global carrier network with proprietary tracking technology to give every client full visibility and control over their supply chain.

Whether you're shipping a single parcel or managing a multi-container trade lane, our operations team works around the clock to keep your cargo moving — safely, compliantly, and on time.

Milestones

Key Turning Points & Achievements

January

2022

Shiprion is founded following several years of research at the Technical University of Munich, focused on optimizing flight control systems for kites used in vessel propulsion.

Between

2023-2024

Prototype trials with a 1.5-ton kite-supported leisure boat at European coasts inform the design of our first full-scale cargo ship. Based on these insights, we’re targeting sub-500 TEU capacity and a kite-only configuration—positioning ourselves for strong cost competitiveness in intra-continental shipping.

October

2024

Tender design finalized for our first full-scale wind-powered vessel—a ~300 TEU catamaran tailored for intra-European cargo transport

December

2024

Feasibility studies with leading shipping and logistics partners simulate Shiprion vessel integration into existing fleets. Using a digital twin and historical weather data, we benchmark global route performance against incumbent vessels—factoring in technical and economic variables like speed, kite efficiency, build costs, and scheduling.

April

2025

Land-based trials of the autonomous flight control system of our kite system as an essential step before integration aboard a vessel

May

2025

Test campaign launched with Shiprion Windora—a 26 m workboat closely aligned with the architecture of our first commercial vessel. It serves as the platform for validating fully automated handling of mid-size kite systems (50–200 m²) and developing the software backbone for autonomous fleet operations.

September

2025

Hydrodynamic testing of a 4.2 m scale model of our first commercial  vessel completed at the Hamburg Ship Model Test Basin (HSVA). The trials validated operational conditions and fuel consumption, providing critical data to de-risk full-scale development.
