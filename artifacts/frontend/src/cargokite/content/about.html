It is also a platform to demonstrate latest technologies and innovations, as well as to facilitate networking and collaboration among all stakeholders.

Introduction

Kiteboarding & Sailing gave us a visceral feel for the power of wind—and the inspiration to harness it

We show the world it's possible.

CargoKite is a maritime hard tech company based in Munich, Germany. The company was founded in 2022 with the mission of sustainable and at the same time economically viable commercial shipping.

We are convinced that software solutions alone won't suffice to decarbonize the shipping industry. We have therefore developed an entirely new ship class which uses the wind as its main source of propulsion to transport goods to any port in the world.

Milestones

Key Turning Points & Achievements

January

2022

CargoKite is founded following several years of research at the Technical University of Munich, focused on optimizing flight control systems for kites used in vessel propulsion.

Between

2023-2024

Prototype trials with a 1.5-ton kite-supported leisure boat at European coasts inform the design of our first full-scale cargo ship. Based on these insights, we’re targeting sub-500 TEU capacity and a kite-only configuration—positioning ourselves for strong cost competitiveness in intra-continental shipping.

October

2024

Tender design finalized for our first full-scale wind-powered vessel—a ~300 TEU catamaran tailored for intra-European cargo transport

December

2024

Feasibility studies with leading shipping and logistics partners simulate CargoKite vessel integration into existing fleets. Using a digital twin and historical weather data, we benchmark global route performance against incumbent vessels—factoring in technical and economic variables like speed, kite efficiency, build costs, and scheduling.

April

2025

Land-based trials of the autonomous flight control system of our kite system as an essential step before integration aboard a vessel

May

2025

Test campaign launched with CargoKite Windora—a 26 m workboat closely aligned with the architecture of our first commercial vessel. It serves as the platform for validating fully automated handling of mid-size kite systems (50–200 m²) and developing the software backbone for autonomous fleet operations.

September

2025

Hydrodynamic testing of a 4.2 m scale model of our first commercial  vessel completed at the Hamburg Ship Model Test Basin (HSVA). The trials validated operational conditions and fuel consumption, providing critical data to de-risk full-scale development.
